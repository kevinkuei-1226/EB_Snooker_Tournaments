Mentorship

If professional coaching is not an option, I am also available for mentorship and guidance for players who want to improve their game. Whether you’re stuck on a scoring plateau, battling inconsistent cueing, or just wanting to make your time at the table more enjoyable, let’s work on it together through 1-on-1 Snooker Mentorship & Game Optimization. The goal is to help you understand more about your game and identify areas for improvement.

It is important to note that I am not a professional coach, but do have experience in playing and analyzing snooker games, and I can provide guidance solely based on my knowledge and experience.

Sessions run for a focused, 45-minute block at a standard rate of $40 (with a full-hour session for the first session to assess skill level), and table time will be paid by the player as well. If you want a friendly sparring partner to help analyze your play and boost your break-building confidence, catch me around the hall or reach out to me at kevinkiwi147@gmail.com for more details, thank you!
